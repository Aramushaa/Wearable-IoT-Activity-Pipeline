Gate Army: Five Empires
Privacy policy

Developer: Sleepy Panda Games
Contact: youaramusha@gmail.com
Last updated: September 5, 2026

This policy describes the browser game and the planned ad-supported Android release. The local browser preview does not load advertising. Advertising is available only in the Android version, when configured and permitted.

Game information stored on your device

The game saves completed missions, medals, best squad size, your chosen outfit, and any earned reinforcement bonus on your device. The Android version also stores whether you selected “under 18” or “18 or older.” We do not ask for your exact date of birth. There are no player accounts, cloud saves, chat, location permissions, purchases, or publisher-operated analytics in this version.

Optional advertising

Players who select “under 18” play without advertising. For adult players, the Android version uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to handle advertising privacy choices. Where required, the app requests permission before it initializes advertising or loads an ad. Eligible adult players may choose to watch a rewarded ad for 15 soldiers on the next run. Watching an ad is not required to play.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, information about app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. This can happen when advertising is initialized or loaded, even if you do not finish watching an ad. Google states that its SDK encrypts this data in transit. Ad availability and personalization depend on your region, settings, and the consent flow.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure for its data handling. This version does not add third-party mediation networks.

Your choices

You can always skip rewarded ads and keep playing. Use “Ad privacy choices” in the Android app to reopen Google's privacy form where it is required and available. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in Android's privacy settings. To delete locally saved game progress and the age-group setting, clear the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. In the browser, clear this site's stored data. The Android app disables cloud backup of its local data.

Young players

The game is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13. This release disables advertising for every player who selects “under 18.” Please contact us if you believe we have received personal information from a child.

Support messages and retention

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and retain it only as needed for that support conversation or legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing us. Local game data remains until you clear it. Google controls retention of data handled by its advertising services; see its linked policies.

Updates

We may revise this policy as features or services change. The date above identifies the current version. Material changes affecting advertising or privacy will be reflected here and, where required, in the app's consent flow.
