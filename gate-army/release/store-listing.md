## Full description

Start with a small squad. Choose your gates. Become a legend.

Gate Army: Five Empires is a quick, colorful army runner built around the gameplay you came to play. Slide left and right to choose number gates, multiply your soldiers, dodge moving blades, and rally your army in the final battle.

LEAD FIVE DISTINCT ARMIES
March as Roman legionaries, Greek hoplites, Persian Immortals, Egyptian Sun Guards, or Norse shieldbearers. Each outfit has its own armor, shield, weapon, and standard. Match the island automatically or choose your favorite from the wardrobe.

RECLAIM FIVE ISLANDS
Cross marble causeways, approach a Greek acropolis, push toward a Persian-inspired palace, battle beside pyramids, and conquer a northern fjord. Fictional rulers and short mission briefings connect your campaign.

MAKE EVERY SOLDIER COUNT
Rescue allies, collect shields, chain growth gates, and time your rally strike. Win missions and earn extra medals for rescue goals and surviving soldiers. Replay unlocked islands to improve your results.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Enjoy touch controls, original music, optional sound effects, pause and restart controls, and locally saved progress. The core game works offline. Advertising requires an internet connection.

OPTIONAL AD REWARDS
Eligible adult players can choose to watch a rewarded ad for 15 extra soldiers on their next run. Ads are never required to play. Players who select an age group under 18 play without ads.

The armies and rulers are stylized fantasy interpretations inspired by different cultures, rather than historical reconstructions.

## Initial release notes
